Six Techniques for Developing a Growth Mindset:
	1. Stop failing and start learning
		Analysis can help you develop a growth mindset, allowing you to see a wider perspective.
	2. You don't need others' approval. You need their criticism.
		Improvement over praise. 
	3. Track your progress with a journal
		Did you like your results? If not, what steps could you take to get  closer to your goal?
	4. Focus inwardly, and not on what you look like to others.
		Think of life as a meritocracy. You do not need other's approval, as you are trying to produce quality work.
	5. Learn and grow from the mistakes of others.
		Don't compare yourself to others, but analyze the mistakes others make so as to avoid making them yourself.
	6. Practice Gratitude
		Counteract self pity and negative thinking with gratitude. Thank the people around you, find things you enjoy about your surroundings, love the world. Send people a thank you note after they meet with you. "Fear leads to anger. Anger leads to hate. Hate leads to suffering." - Yoda

	7 Steps for problem solving:
		1. Identifying the problem. You need to break the problem down and establish the end goal and performance objectives of solving the problem.
		2. Define the goals. How would you define the problem being solved?
You are their to make life easier, not to alleviate all their problems. (Make the margin of error smaller)	
		3. Brainstorm.Get together with friends, get Windows Paint out, and brainstorm solutions. Make sure nobody feels uncomfortable. No answer is a bad one. Ask for half an idea, maybe someone will have the other half. Add more viewpoints, gain more perspective.
		4. Assess your alternative solutions. Will your solutions defy physics? How much time and money will it save people?
		5. Selecting a solution. You probably will not get it first try.
		6. Monitor the results of your product in the market. Analyze your data well, and fast. Reacting to the market is very important.
		7. Evaluate the solution, the market, and your competition. The first person you will educate is your competition.

		-"Let them ridicule you, laugh at you, hurt you, and ignore you, but never let them stop you." - Apoorve Dubey

			Financial security, ability to fund the venture, personal ability, potential of the idea, threats to self esteem, venture's ability to execute, and lost opportunity costs.

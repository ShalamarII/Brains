You are sunlight. You are sunlight and I don't care if you can't see it because the stick in my eye won't stop me from seeing the light in yours. I would break every bone in my body to show you who you are to me. I'd grind myself to dust to know you could see yourself as I do. I wish I could capture it, some of your light. If I did I could bring peace to a war-torn land, life to the dead, memory to those who've forgotten. If I had a bit of your light I could sing a note so pure it'd bring my heart to a still. It would sound of hugs given and hands held; of late nights cooking and dancing, of ice cream melted and hands fumbling; of friendship and fear. It would blind me, that bit of light. It would imprint itself behind my eyes and I would not care. You are not so simple that I can describe you with words, but I will try.

You are fire and it feels so infinitely reductive to try and represent even one iota of who you are. You rend thought from my mind, forcing me to action that I might see you. When I look upon you, I glimpse the soul of humanity. It strikes me dumb and I feel that is beautiful. I am always writing to you, hoping you can hear me. I lay traps only you can understand and tell stories only you would find funny. I am writing to you, speaking and hoping you will hear. I am terrified I have broken us, that we cannot fix us. That I killed the greatest child ever born. Every bit of water in my body is coming out from my eyes writing this. My head is pounding and my throat is closing and writing is the only thing I can do. I am sure I will struggle to stand, to drink, to sleep. But it is nothing. It cannot hold even one trillionth of the feeling behind each word of this. In truth, I would scream this at you. That you would hear it and it would be all you could think about. That I might be carried in your mind some way, even if not with love. That your light might hold some bit of who we were together. I love you. I love you and its killing me. It is eating away at the shame I feel and the distrust in my actions. You reassure me that I am not alone, that I can hold a spark. In my mind you hold me and remind me that life is beautiful. My throat rasps and my teeth shatter as I write this. There is no corner of my soul, no instinct in my body that can do anything else but love you. I am sorry for leading you to believe anything else.

I wrote a story today. It was a little thing, a story about a man talking all day with his husband. It was a trifling thing, something small and hopeful. I saw myself within him as he smiled, something in the way he sipped his coffee, the drink a bit too hot for his taste. I saw myself reflected in my writing; and I saw myself reflected in the coffee. A dirty thing you must learn to tolerate. A distillation of something that when done wrong makes a man walk away.

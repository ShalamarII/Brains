Shame is an ugly thing, a cavernous bubble that forms itself about my mind,. Shame is said to be important, to be a sign of those who feel as though something is wrong. But that is embarrassment, not the bone deep gouging of the soul that shame requires. When my shame is felt, I myself harken towards that eternal darkness, that I might escape this mind of mine. But shame passes, and embarrassment rises, and I am alone again.

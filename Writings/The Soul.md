There is something about the soul that cannot be measured. It exists in our art and in our words, in stories untold and memories long forgotten. If humanity were to disappear today a piece of that soul would remain there. You could walk into houses and be told a story of the people who lived there, who loved there. The world is chaos, but you can still see it. We find beauty in the mundane, love in those who see themselves unfit. We can't help it. It is our very nature itself. It is humanity. When we say people lack humanity, do we mean empathy? Or is it this instinct, this beckoning to find meaning in the meaningless, to find light in a dark room. Is it perseverance, that fight in us since birth, to reach out, to hold something? Or is it kindness, that we would extend a hand to one another. Is it creativity, that we take a part of ourselves and put it in our works? We stare out into blank space imagining landscapes beyond ourselves. We paint that we should see life in the cobbled stones of a worn street and color in murals washed grey. In all we do, we shout. We laugh. We cry. I believe "humanity" is a matter of perspective. Evil does not see itself in need. It is similarly perturbed by those it cannot understand. But even Evil puts a part of itself in the things it creates. It builds grand ballrooms, palaces, and has statues erected in its' name. Evil would hear that people love it, that it is remarkable. Evil does this in the hope that it too might be captured, a bit of its soul. That someone may look upon that statue in times lost and see some bit of what it believed it was. That someone can look upon that statue in times lost and see some bit of what it was. Evil is frail, just like us all. Evil too does not deserve to be riddled with pain in the hopes that it will be recognized, that it can be safe. In saying this, I am a hypocrite. I too reach out, seeking the safety it is to be known. I too seek to be remarkable. As much as I know it is futile, it is all I know. Even still, I am trying. I am inching forwards ever so slowly, with great effort. And I believe that you should too. We should not let the world tell us it is a crime to be human, to be anything but a tool, an implement of productivity. We cannot forget one another under incentive, else we may become that very evil that we may despise.

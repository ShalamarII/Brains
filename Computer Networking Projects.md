#### Mini PC NAS
Items Needed:
- Mini PC (Old Chromebox or HP SFF PCs  are good budget options. You can also opt for a NUC. You can Rico if you need help.)
- Compatible SSD (Recommend 500GB to 1TB)

Recommended Software:
- Debian Linux (Stable Release, LTS is NOT worth it)
- MobaXTerm
- OpenSSH
- Docker
- Immich (for Photo Storage)
- Playit.gg (for exposing to the internet)'

For this, you'll do research on 3-5 programs (https://github.com/awesome-selfhosted/awesome-selfhosted) that you can self-host on a low-powered NAS. The beauty of computers is that you REALLY don't need amazing hardware to do pretty amazing stuff.

If you don't understand how ports work or are allocated, it's time to look into that now.
